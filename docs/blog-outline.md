# Technical article outline

The final article will follow an actual research run from idea to evidence, including a failed
experiment. It will explain the boundary between model proposals and deterministic research
checks, the point-in-time and portfolio-accounting assumptions, independent engine verification,
and the cost of preserving reproducibility.

Results, baseline comparisons, and deployment lessons will be added after the corresponding
experiments have been run. No target metric is presented as a completed result.
