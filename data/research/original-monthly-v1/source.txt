Original score strategy

This is an original software integration fixture, not a published equity factor.
At the last trading session close of each month, rank historically eligible
securities by the current dimensionless input named score. The signal formula is
score. There is no trailing return lookback. The fixed formation lag is zero months.
Divide securities into two buckets using all eligible securities, with stable
security-ID tie ordering. Go long the high-score bucket and short the low-score
bucket, weighting securities equally within each bucket. Reconstitute monthly
and hold each cohort for one month, without overlapping cohorts. Trade at the next
session open. Formation rule: Last session close each month.

The accompanying original two-security data uses a declared UTC weekday calendar,
raw prices, six basis points of commission and four basis points of slippage.
These fixture values demonstrate reproducible accounting and source extraction;
they establish no empirical return, factor-reproduction or investment claim.
